📋 Assignments Grid

Click a cell to assign/unassign the selected part. Each part shows its short code; gold-edged codes are Students-tab parts. A red ring means the brother also has a part the meeting before or after. The light trail runs up to the last time he had the selected part — no trail = never had it.
